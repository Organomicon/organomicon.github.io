## Overview
The aldol reaction forms β‑hydroxy carbonyl compounds via enolate addition.

## Mechanism
1. Enolate formation  
2. Nucleophilic attack  
3. Aldol formation  

## Variants
- Aldol condensation  
- Crossed aldol  
- Directed aldol  

## Historical Note
Named after the product “aldol” (aldehyde + alcohol).
